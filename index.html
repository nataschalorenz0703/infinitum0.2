TATTOO ARTISTS

ARS

OLD SCHOOL

Meet Ars, who has traveled all the way from his base in Berlin to guest at the event. Ars works primarily within the classic, traditional old-school style.

BLACKWORK

Based in Copenhagen, Laura Joseph focuses exclusively on intricate blackwork. Her style blends sharp lines with heavy dark shading, creating timeless, high-impact pieces.

NEO TRADITIONAL

Working out of Berlin, Nastia pushes the boundaries of neo-traditional tattooing with fearless use of color. Her designs balance intricate detailing with heavy, saturated tones that stand out in the dark..

Poli is a Copenhagen based tattoo artist specializing in raw, experimental abstract work. Breaking away from conventional forms, his pieces move organically across the skin like spontaneous, dark ink compositions..
